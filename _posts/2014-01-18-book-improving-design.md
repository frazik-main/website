## Introduction

This book, "Refactoring: Improving the Design of Existing Code," is excellent reading for anyone looking to enhance their coding skills with proven techniques. While many of these concepts are foundational, knowing them by name and understanding their structured application is invaluable for improving code quality and maintainability.

Happy reading!
